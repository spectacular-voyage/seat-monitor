Tiers and Codex renewals come from each provider; billing checks run at most once every six hours per account and stay on this device. Claude does not report renewal dates or cancellations to Claude Code, so a Claude date marked est. is the monthly anniversary of the subscription start. To record a Claude billing day or a cancellation, add "billing": {"renewalDay": 14, "autoRenew": false} to that account in accounts.json.
